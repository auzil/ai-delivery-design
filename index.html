Questions couriers ask

Something not here? Ask in chat, we'll answer before you apply.

Ask on WhatsApp
What happens if the scooter breaks down?

Message us where you are. We bring a replacement scooter, so you don't lose the shift. Service and repairs from normal wear are included.

What if I have an accident?

First make sure you and others are safe, then take photos and message us straight away. We'll take you through the insurance steps.

What insurance is included?

Every scooter is insured. We send you a summary of the cover in chat before you sign, so you know what is and isn't covered.

Is there a deposit? What about damage?

There is a deposit, paid at pickup and returned when you bring the scooter back in the same condition. We tell you the amount in chat before you apply.

How do I pay?

In advance, for the week or the month. We'll agree card or bank transfer at pickup.

Can I extend or stop early?

Extend by message before your period ends. If you need to stop early, tell us in chat and we'll explain the terms before you sign.

Who pays for fuel?

You do. Use the calculator above to see what fuel costs you per week.

Can I use the scooter outside shifts?

Yes, it's yours for the whole rental period, on and off shift.

Which platforms can I work for?

Any: Wolt, Bolt Food, foodora or others. The rent is a flat price, nothing is taken from your earnings.
